About ArtZi

ArtZi is a quiet, carefully designed space dedicated to rehearsals. We invest in acoustics, equipment and comfort so every hour you spend here is productive.

Whether you're a solo musician or a full band, you can bring ideas, work on pre-production or simply play loud without limits.

What you’ll find

  • Acoustically treated rooms
  • Professional drums, amps & PA
  • Air‑conditioning and comfortable lighting
  • Easy access & parking

Studios & Equipment

Studio A

Medium-sized room ideal for a full band with drums. Balanced sound and a comfortable layout.

  • 5‑piece drum kit
  • Guitar & bass amplifiers
  • PA with microphones

Studio B

More compact room for smaller groups, vocal rehearsals or pre-production.

  • Full-range monitor system
  • Line in for laptop / interface
  • Small lounge area

Extras

Everything you need to feel comfortable during your rehearsal.

  • Wi‑Fi & power outlets
  • Coffee / water
  • Waiting area
