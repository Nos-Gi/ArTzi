About ArtZi

ArtZi is a quiet, carefully designed space dedicated to rehearsals. We invest in acoustics, equipment and comfort so every hour you spend here is productive.

Whether you're a solo musician or a full band, you can bring ideas, work on pre-production or simply play loud without limits.

What you’ll find

  • Acoustically treated rooms
  • Professional drums, amps & PA
  • Air‑conditioning and comfortable lighting
  • Easy access & parking
